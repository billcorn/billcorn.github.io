FAQ

Quick answers before you try it.

How much does Billcorn cost?

Billcorn has one plan at ₹1,999/year. You can create 10 sample bills with a watermark before starting a 1 month free trial or subscribing. The paid plan includes unlimited bill generation and 3 active sessions across devices.

Can I create a bill without adding inventory first?

Yes. Billcorn is made for fast billing, so you can create a bill immediately without importing inventory first.

Can I send a bill on WhatsApp without saving the number?

Yes. Enter the customer number and Billcorn opens WhatsApp for that number with a shareable bill link, so you do not have to create a contact first.

Does Billcorn support thermal printer invoices?

Yes. You can print receipts on thermal printers and also print regular A4 invoices when needed.

Can I create barcode labels?

Yes. Create barcodes for products without existing codes, print labels, stick them, and scan them during checkout.

Can I generate recurring or subscription bills?

Yes. Billcorn supports recurring billing for services and plans measured by hour, day, week, month, or year.
