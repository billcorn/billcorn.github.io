BILLCORN PRIVACY

Billcorn (“Billcorn”, “we”, “our”, “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy applies to our marketing website and any Billcorn products and services that link to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information you provide

  • Account information (e.g., name, email, phone, business name) when you sign up or contact us.
  • Billing and payment details when you subscribe (typically processed by a payment provider).
  • Support content you send us (messages, attachments, or feedback).

B. Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage data (e.g., IP address, browser type, pages viewed, referral source).
  • Cookies and similar technologies for essential functions, analytics, and improving user experience.

C. Information from third parties

  • We may receive limited data from service providers (e.g., payment processors, analytics) as necessary to operate our services.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Billcorn services.
  • Create and manage accounts, authenticate users, and process transactions.
  • Respond to inquiries and provide customer support.
  • Improve features, performance, and user experience.
  • Send service-related messages (e.g., updates, security notices) and marketing (where permitted).
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, or security incidents.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

3. Legal Bases

Where required by law, we process personal information under one or more legal bases such as: (a) performance of a contract, (b) legitimate interests, (c) consent, or (d) legal compliance.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers that help us operate (hosting, analytics, customer support, payment processing).
  • Legal and safety authorities when required by law or to protect rights and safety.
  • Business transfers in connection with mergers, acquisitions, or asset sales.

5. Cookies

Cookies help us deliver the service and improve performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect site functionality.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as necessary for legitimate business purposes and legal requirements, and then delete or anonymize it when no longer needed.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access, update, or delete your information (subject to legal exceptions).
  • Opt out of marketing communications (via unsubscribe links or contacting us).
  • Cookie controls via your browser settings.

9. Children’s Privacy

Billcorn is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. International Transfers

If you access Billcorn from outside our operating country, your information may be transferred and processed in locations where data protection laws may differ.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We’ll update the “Last updated” date above and, when appropriate, provide additional notice.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions or requests about privacy, contact us at: contact@billcorn.com
